**$65 CAD per practitioner per month.** Sales tax (HST / GST / PST) is
added on top of the $65, calculated based on your billing address. There are no tiers, no per-feature add-ons, no
pay-as-you-call usage charges. Cancel anytime.

## What's included

Everything Rivet does. The same list as the trial:

- **Your practice phone line** — a local Canadian phone number, with
  unlimited inbound and outbound calls inside North America, unlimited
  SMS inside North America (inbound from anywhere — clients can text
  you from abroad), and voicemail transcription on every missed call.
- **Two-way SMS** with auto-replies, per-category controls, and the
  conversation inbox.
- **Video sessions** — your permanent waiting room at
  `getrivet.ca/your-slug` and on-demand session links. No participant
  cap on group sessions inside reasonable practice limits, no minutes
  meter, no per-session fee.
- **Clinical templates** — the full library of intake, screener, and
  session templates plus the per-practitioner customisation surface.
- **EMDR** — bilateral stimulation with visual and audio cues, set
  history, the 2.0 Hz speed cap, and the photo-epilepsy warning.
- **Session notes + per-session PDF export.**
- **Measurement-based-care templates** — sending measures async or
  in-session, scoring, severity bands, trajectories.
- **App + browser push notifications** — desktop browsers (Chrome,
  Safari, Firefox), iOS, Android.
- **Biometric unlock** on phones.
- **A real human at the other end of hello@getrivet.ca.**

## What isn't included

Two things, both for good reasons:

- **International outbound calls and SMS** outside North America. Your
  Rivet line dials and texts inside Canada and the US. Inbound from
  abroad is fine — clients can call or text you from anywhere. The
  outbound restriction is a fraud-mitigation choice; the per-minute
  rates to some international destinations are how telecom abuse
  bills get to four figures overnight.
- **Toll-free numbers**. Rivet provisions local Canadian numbers, not
  1-800-style toll-free. Local numbers have better SMS deliverability
  and read as more personal to a Canadian client; toll-free numbers
  have neither advantage and a longer carrier-registration timeline.

If you have a use case for either — say, your clients are spread across
provinces and want a toll-free option, or you have international
clients you call regularly — email us. We can usually find a way.

## Why the model is flat

One price means a few things:

- **No friction at the moment of needing a feature.** A practitioner
  in the middle of a session shouldn't be reading "Upgrade to Pro to
  use clinical templates" pop-ups. Either the feature exists for
  everyone or it doesn't exist.
- **No tier math at signup.** You sign up, you have all of Rivet. The
  decision is "yes or no," not "yes, and which tier?"
- **No surprise overage charges.** A busy practice with 200
  voicemails in a month pays the same as a sleepy practice with 10.
  No call is too expensive to make.
- **Easier for your accountant.** One line on every invoice
  for the whole year.

The trade-off is that we don't charge more for heavy use. We're
betting that the kind of practice that grows into heavy use is also
the kind that tells colleagues about Rivet, and we'd rather earn
the next practitioner than nickel-and-dime the current one.

## Comparing to other tools

For context — you'd typically pay more for the parts taken separately:

- A dedicated practice phone line (OpenPhone, Google Voice, etc.):
  $15–$30 / month.
- A telehealth video platform (Doxy, SimplePractice video, Jane
  video add-on): $25–$50 / month.
- A clinical-template library tied to your EHR: usually bundled into
  $40–$100 / month EHR pricing.
- EMDR-specific software (RemoteEMDR, Bilateral Base): $20–$30 / month.

The premise is that none of those tools alone solves the whole "I am
a solo practitioner running a practice over the phone, by text, by
video, with notes" problem. Rivet's $65 covers all of it.

## Group practices

Rivet doesn't have a published group-rate tier — every account is
billed independently at $65 / month / practitioner. That said, if
you're a practice with multiple practitioners and you'd like
consolidated billing or a multi-seat conversation, email us. We
handle these case-by-case rather than ship a multi-seat model that
isn't yet earning its complexity.
