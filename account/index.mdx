Your account is one practitioner, one practice. Everything in this section
lives in the same place in the app — open the slide-in **account panel** by
tapping your avatar in the top-right of any screen.

The panel has the controls you reach for most often: your sign-in identity,
your Rivet phone number, your auto-reply state, browser notifications
(desktop), biometric unlock (phone), the Data Processing Agreement you
accepted, and the **Sign Out** button. Anything that isn't there is handled
by emailing **hello@getrivet.ca** — a real human reads every message.

## How identity works in Rivet

Three layers, each separate so a change in one doesn't break the others:

- **Identity** — your email address. The magic-link sign-in confirms that
  you control it. There is no password.
- **Trust for the session** — the magic link itself, plus (on phones)
  Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint if you've turned **Biometric Unlock**
  on in the account panel.
- **Convenience** — your session stays signed in for 90 days of activity.
  Open Rivet at least once a quarter and you'll never see a sign-in
  screen.

If you ever want to start fresh — say, you're handing the phone over to
your kid for an afternoon — tap **Sign Out** in the account panel. The
session clears, the biometric preference resets, and the next launch
starts from the sign-in screen.
