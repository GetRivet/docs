---
title: "Hide what your notifications show"
description: "Choose whether notifications on your phone's lock screen show who it's from and the first words, or only that something arrived."
---

You can choose what Rivet's notifications show. By default they show who it's from and, for a text, the first words of it. If you'd rather a glance at your phone, or a screen in the room, reveal nothing about your clients, switch the previews off.

## Can I hide what my notifications show?

Yes. It's one switch.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
2. Turn **Message previews** off to hide them, or on to show them.

On your computer, the switch sits just under the browser notifications switch.

It's one setting for your whole practice, so it applies on every phone and computer you use Rivet on.

## What you'll see

With previews **shown**, notifications read as they always have. A text shows the number it came from and the first words of the message. A voicemail shows the caller's number and what the call was about. Waiting room and form notifications show the client's name.

With previews **hidden**, the notification names only the kind of thing that arrived:

| What happened | Notification title | Notification text |
|---|---|---|
| A client texted | New message | Open Rivet to see it. |
| Someone left a voicemail | New voicemail | Open Rivet to see it. |
| A call was missed | Missed call | Open Rivet to see it. |
| A client is in the waiting room | Client waiting | Open Rivet to see it. |
| A form was completed | Form completed | Open Rivet to see it. |

Tap the notification and Rivet opens, where you'll see the full message.

## The text alerts to your phone too

If you've set Rivet to text your own phone when something arrives, those texts follow the same setting. With previews hidden, they read "Rivet: New voicemail." (or whichever kind it is), followed by the usual link. No name, number or words from the client.

If Rivet can't check your setting at that moment, it hides the details.

## The card that asks once

Rivet asks once, with a card: **Choose what notifications show**, with **Show previews** and **Hide previews**. It starts on Show previews, which is how your notifications worked before. Pick one and tap Continue. You won't be asked again.

If Rivet couldn't save your choice, it tells you "Couldn't save. We'll ask again next time." and asks again the next time you open it. You can always change it later in **Settings**, then **Notifications**.

Changes apply to the next notification. One already sitting on your phone keeps the words it arrived with.
