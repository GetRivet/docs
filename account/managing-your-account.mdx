Your account controls are in **Settings**, on your computer and on your phone. Auto-Reply, Greeting and Forwarding have their own tabs.

## On your computer

Click your picture or initials, or your practice name, at the bottom of the left menu. If you've collapsed the left menu, click your picture or initials. A short menu opens with Do Not Disturb, your working hours, search, and shortcuts into Settings. Turning Do Not Disturb on asks for the message clients get while it's on. Turning it off is one click.

**Settings** has its own page, at the bottom of the left menu or in that menu:

- **Practice number**: your number, and links to Auto-Reply, Greeting and Forwarding. If your number is moving to Rivet, you can follow the move from here.
- **Do Not Disturb**: the switch and the message clients get while it's on.
- **Working hours**: your whole week, and where to change it.
- **Plan & billing**: your plan, the days left in your trial, and a link to your receipts and card on file.
- **Legal**: the Data Processing Agreement you accepted, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
- **Profile**: how you sign in and your practice name. Your photo and Rivet URL are in the Waiting Room.
- **Notifications**: alerts in this browser.
- **Help & support**: the product guide and email support, plus the setup guide while you're still setting up.

**Sign out** is at the bottom of the menu. It signs you out on all your devices.

## On your phone

Tap your avatar in the top-right corner of any screen. A short menu slides in from the right:

- **Who you are:** the email address you sign in with, your practice name, and whether you're in office, on Do Not Disturb or outside your hours. While you're on a trial, the days left show here too.
- **Do Not Disturb:** turning it on asks for the message clients get while it's on. Keep your last message, change it or clear it, then tap **Turn on**. Turning it off is one tap, and your message stays saved for next time.
- **Working hours:** shows your hours. Tap to change them, or to set them if you haven't yet.
- **Your practice number,** with a button to share it.
- **Settings**, **Invite a colleague** once you've finished setting up, and **Help & support**.

**Settings** opens a full screen:

- **Practice number:** your number, and links to Auto-Reply, Greeting and Forwarding.
- **Do Not Disturb:** the switch and your message. The message only goes out while Do Not Disturb is on, once per conversation. Leave it empty and clients get your regular auto-reply.
- **Working hours:** your whole week, and where to change it. Outside these hours, calls go straight to voicemail and you aren't notified. Callers still get your regular auto-reply.
- **Plan & billing:** where to find your plan and receipts. Those are at next.getrivet.ca.
- **Legal:** the Data Processing Agreement you accepted, the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
- **Profile:** how you sign in and your practice name.
- **Face ID** (or Touch ID, or fingerprint): turn it on, and Rivet asks for it right away to confirm it works. It only stays on if that succeeds.
- **Help & support:** the product guide and email support, plus the setup guide while you're still setting up.
- **Sign out**, at the bottom. It signs you out on all your devices, and clears the Face ID setting and saved data on this phone.

## The settings tabs

Reachable from **More** in the bottom bar on your phone, or the left menu on your computer:

- **Auto-Reply** — turn each voicemail category's automatic reply on or
  off, and edit the reply text per category.
- **Greeting** — your voicemail greeting. Use the default, record your
  own, or upload an audio file.
- **Forwarding** — instructions for forwarding your existing practice
  number to Rivet, organised by carrier.

These are about how Rivet handles calls, not about your account
identity. A new colleague borrowing your laptop and signing in as
themselves should see your Auto-Reply text wiped clean — because it
belongs to *the account*, not *the laptop*.

## Where things are NOT

A few controls you might look for that don't exist as in-app surfaces:

- **Change your email address** — not in the app. Email
  hello@getrivet.ca and we'll re-bind your account.
- **Change your phone number (the Rivet line)** — not in the app.
  See [Changing your Rivet number](/account/changing-your-number).
- **Billing and invoices** — handled through the billing portal, not the Rivet app. See
  [Invoices and receipts](/billing/invoices-and-receipts).
- **Cancel your subscription** — handled by emailing us. See
  [Canceling your account](/account/canceling-your-account).

This is intentional. We'd rather a human-touched cancellation, billing,
or number change than build a dashboard that you'll use once a year.
