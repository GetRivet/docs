Rivet is the session app for therapists. Video sessions, EMDR, clinical
templates, whiteboard, practitioner-private notes. Plus the practice phone
underneath — your business number, voicemail inbox, two-way texting, and
auto-response — so the calls and texts a client sends between sessions land in
the same place as the session itself.

This guide is the reference for using Rivet day-to-day. Short pages, plain
language, organised by what you're actually trying to do.

<Note>
  Stuck on something not covered here? Email **hello@getrivet.ca** — one
  business-day response, real human.
</Note>
