---
title: "Clinical templates"
description: "Standardized measures, therapeutic worksheets, and your own private session notes — all inside the session, with live scoring."
---

Clinical templates are the structured forms you reach for during a session — a
PHQ-9 at intake, a thought record mid-session, a SOAP note while the client
talks. Open a measure in a video session and your client fills it on their
device while you watch the score rise on yours. Send one to a client ahead of
a session and it's filled before you sit down together. Or fill worksheets and
private notes yourself while the client talks. Everything saves to the client's
record when you're done.
