The future template is Phase 8 — the final piece of Shapiro's
three-pronged protocol. Past targets are reprocessed, present triggers
are desensitized, and then the client rehearses an anticipated future
situation, holding the positive cognition and the adaptive behavior,
with BLS reinforcing the rehearsal.

This template captures the situation, the adaptive behavior, the
positive cognition, and the pre/post VOC of the installation.

## What the template covers

Two sections:

| Section | What you capture |
|---|---|
| **Anticipated future situation** | Situation description, adaptive behavior, positive cognition |
| **Installation** | VOC before, BLS sets, VOC after, any-disturbance flag, notes |

## When to use it

Phase 8 is typically the final phase of EMDR treatment for a given
target or theme. Use the future template when:

- **Past targets are reprocessed.** Phase 4 was completed for the
  contributing past memories.
- **Present triggers are desensitized.** Day-to-day reactivity has
  settled.
- **The client has an anticipated situation** where the issue might
  arise — a public-speaking event, a family gathering, a return to a
  place that's been avoided.

The future template rehearses the desired adaptive response in advance,
so the client has installed a behavioral plan before the situation
arrives.

## How to launch it

In the EMDR Workspace or from the templates picker, search "future
template." Fill the worksheet in order — situation, behavior, positive
cognition, then the installation log.

The BLS itself runs in the [EMDR Workspace](/emdr/setting-up-an-emdr-session).

## Field-by-field

### Anticipated future situation

- **Situation description** — large text field. Specific, concrete,
  near-future. "Going to my sister's wedding next month and seeing my
  ex." Not "any situation where I might feel anxious."
- **Adaptive behavior** — large text field. How the client wants to
  respond. Behavior, not feeling. "Stay at the wedding, keep
  conversations short with my ex, sit at my sister's table." The
  behavior is the rehearsal target.
- **Positive cognition** — short string. The PC to install with the
  rehearsal. Same form as Phase 5 PC — "I can handle this," "I am
  capable," "I have choices."

### Installation

- **VOC before** — slider 1–7. Validity-of-cognition rating before the
  rehearsal sets begin. Sets a baseline.
- **BLS sets completed** — integer. Cumulative sets across the
  installation. Short slow sets, same pacing as Phase 5 installation
  work.
- **VOC after** — slider 1–7. Validity-of-cognition rating after the
  installation. The change is the outcome.
- **Any disturbance** — boolean. Did disturbance surface during the
  rehearsal? If yes, you've found a new target.
- **Notes** — large text field, optional.

## Reading the installation

- **VOC moves to 7** — the future template is installed. Treatment for
  this theme can wind down.
- **VOC stays below 7** — additional past targets or present triggers
  may need work first. The future rehearsal isn't holding because the
  earlier prongs aren't fully clear.
- **Disturbance surfaces during rehearsal** — a new target. Return to
  Phase 3 with the material that surfaced. The future template work
  pauses until the new target is processed.

The third pattern — disturbance surfacing — is common and often
useful. The rehearsal is a controlled exposure; if it surfaces material
the prior work missed, that material is now visible and processable.

## How this fits with the body scan

Body scan (Phase 6) is per-target — you scan after each desensitized
memory. Future template (Phase 8) is per-theme — you rehearse the
anticipated future after the contributing targets are all processed.

A complete arc on one theme looks like:

1. Phase 3 target identification (target memory, NC, PC, VOC, SUDS, body)
2. Phase 4 desensitization (BLS to SUDS = 0)
3. Phase 5 installation (BLS to VOC ≥ 7)
4. Phase 6 body scan (this template's sibling)
5. Phase 7 closure (calm place or container)
6. Phase 8 reevaluation + future template (this template)

Future template is the last step. When the VOC at after-installation is
7 and no disturbance surfaced, the theme is complete.
