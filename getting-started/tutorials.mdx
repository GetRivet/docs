---
title: "Watch a tutorial in Rivet"
description: "Short videos that show how each part of Rivet works. Find them all in Learn, or play one from the page you're on."
---

Rivet has a short video for most things you'll do in it: replying to a
text, setting your working hours, sending a measure. Each one runs a
minute or two and plays inside Rivet on the web, at `next.getrivet.ca`,
on a computer or in your phone's browser.

## Where to find them

**Learn** holds every tutorial. On a computer it's in the left menu,
marked with a play button. On a phone-sized screen, open **Settings**
and choose **Learn**.

Tutorials are grouped by what they're about:

| Section | Tutorials |
|---|---|
| **Start here** | Desktop tour |
| **Phone** | Reply to a text and start one · Inbox: Messages and Calls · Voicemail and transcripts · Auto-reply to missed callers · Do Not Disturb and the away message · Working hours · Contacts · Keep or move your number |
| **Sessions** | Send a measure · The client record and export |
| **Account** | Settings and appearance |

Under the tutorials, **Written guides** opens this guide.

## Play one from the page you're on

Pages that have a tutorial show a line with a small play button, such as
**How auto-reply works** on Auto-Reply. Select it and the tutorial opens
over the page. Close it and you're back where you were.

You'll find these on the Inbox and Contacts when nothing is selected,
and on Auto-Reply, Forwarding, Do Not Disturb, Working hours, Forms, a
client's record and Appearance.

## While you watch

- A tutorial starts when you press play.
- **Transcript** shows the narration as text.
- **Read the guide** opens the written article on the same subject, when
  there is one.
- **All tutorials** takes you to Learn.

Close a tutorial partway and Rivet remembers your place on that device.
Open it again and it picks up from there.

## What comes next

When a tutorial ends, **Up next** shows the next one you haven't seen.
Choose **Play next** to open it, or **Watch again** to replay the one
you just finished.

## Keeping track

A tutorial is marked **Watched** once you've seen it through. Learn
shows how many you've watched, such as "3 of 12 watched", and the count
follows your account, so it's the same on every computer you sign in on.

## The first time you open Rivet on a computer

Rivet offers the **Desktop tour**, a two-minute look around. Choose
**Watch the tour** to play it, or **Not now** to skip it. It stays in
Learn under **Start here**.
