You can keep the phone number your clients already have. Rivet moves it
from your current provider: you upload your latest phone bill, check the
details, and sign, all in a few minutes on your computer. Most moves take
one to three weeks.

<Warning>
Keep your current phone service until we tell you the move is done.
Cancelling it early can lose the number.
</Warning>

## What to have ready

Every move needs your **latest phone bill**. What else depends on the kind
of phone you're bringing.

### A cell phone

- The **transfer PIN** for your account. Your provider can give you this
  in their app or over the phone.
- If your plan is **prepaid**, the phone's IMEI. Dial \*#06# on the phone
  to see it.
- **iPhone:** turn the number off in Messages and FaceTime before the move
  date. **Android:** turn off chat features in Messages. Otherwise
  clients' texts keep going to the old phone.
- **Verification codes** from banks and government services that text this
  number will stop arriving on your phone after the move. Switch those
  accounts to another number first.
- Your phone gets a new number from your provider. If this is also your
  personal cell, share the new one with family and friends.

### A landline

- The **service address** exactly as it's printed on your bill. That's
  where the line is installed, and it can differ from your mailing
  address.
- If your **internet comes on the same line**, it can be cut when the
  number moves. Ask your provider to keep your internet on its own before
  you start.

### An office phone system

- Your account number, and your transfer PIN if your provider uses one.
- Check your contract's cancellation terms.

### A phone app

- Your account number, and a PIN if the app uses one.
- Some apps don't let numbers move, and some charge a fee to release one.
  Psychology Today numbers belong to Psychology Today and can't move.

### For any phone

- Save any voicemails you want to keep. They stay with your old service.
- If the account also has other numbers or internet, the form asks what
  should happen to them: keep them with your provider, move them too, or
  cancel them.

## Upload your bill

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Rivet on your computer">
    Sign in at your computer and open **Messages**. The banner at the top
    says **Upload your bill**. On your phone, tap **Finish on your
    computer** instead and we'll email you a link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your bill">
    A PDF from your provider's website works best. A clear photo of a
    paper bill works too. The file can be up to 10 MB, and you can upload
    up to 10 times a day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait about half a minute">
    Rivet reads your bill on its own computers in Canada. No outside
    service sees it. You can leave the page while it reads; the banner
    says **Your details are ready to check** when it's done. After 45
    seconds you can also choose **Fill it in yourself** and type the
    details in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check and sign

The form opens with what Rivet read from your bill. Anything marked
**Please check this one** is worth a second look. Every detail needs to
match your bill exactly: your provider compares them with their own
records.

1. Check the name, the service address and the account number.
2. Choose your current provider.
3. Type your transfer PIN. If the form offers **My provider doesn't use a
   PIN** and that's true for you, tick it instead.
4. Confirm each statement under **Before you sign**.
5. Sign in the box with your mouse, trackpad or finger, type your full
   name, and choose **Sign and send**.

We prepare the request to your provider and let you know when it's on its
way. Your signed form is valid for 30 days. If it hasn't gone to your
provider within 25 days, we'll email you to **sign again**. Your details
are saved: type your PIN again if your provider uses one, then sign.

## While your provider reviews it

If your number is a cell phone or a phone app, your provider may text you
to approve the move, at any hour. **Reply within 90 minutes**, or the move
is cancelled. Keep your phone with you, and make sure texts from your
provider aren't blocked.

If your provider sends the request back, the banner says so and shows
their reason. Choose **Edit and sign again**, check your details against
your bill, and sign.

Video sessions work the whole time. Clients join from your Rivet link.

## On move day

Calls to your number start ringing in Rivet. Texts usually follow within
minutes, and can take up to 3 business days; until then, some may still
reach your old phone. When calls and texts both work, we'll email you to
say the move is done. Then you can cancel your old phone service.

## 911

Rivet can't call 911. Keep another phone at the office for emergencies,
and set it up before move day.

## Your bill and your form

Your bill and your signed form are kept privately until 30 days after the
move is done or cancelled, then deleted. The details Rivet read from your
bill are deleted as soon as you sign.
