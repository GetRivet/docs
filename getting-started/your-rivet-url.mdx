The first time you open your Waiting Room, it asks you to pick your Rivet
URL. This is the identity moment of setup. The URL you pick —
`getrivet.ca/your-name` — is the single address your clients use for every
session. They save it once, tap it each time, and never need a meeting ID,
a fresh link, or an account login.

It's yours for as long as you're a Rivet customer.

## What your clients see when they visit your URL

Your URL is your **waiting room**. When a client opens
`getrivet.ca/your-name` — from the link in your text, your email, or a
bookmark — they land on a page that shows:

- Your practice name
- A button to join
- Permission requests for their camera and microphone
- A waiting state once they've joined, with a note that you'll let them
  in when you're ready

You see them appear on your side, and you tap to let them into the
session.

The waiting room is always at the same URL. A client could see you weekly
for six months and never ask "What's the link this time?" — it's the same
page, every visit.

## Picking your name

Under the heading **Pick your Rivet URL**, the picker shows `getrivet.ca/`
with a field after it. Type what you want; Rivet checks as you type and
tells you whether it's available.

Some guidance:

- **Use the shortest form of your name that's still recognisable.**
  `getrivet.ca/sarah-chen` reads fine. `getrivet.ca/dr-sarah-elizabeth-chen-rsw`
  is too long to remember and too tall for a business card.
- **Hyphens are allowed, spaces aren't.** Lowercase only. Letters, numbers
  and hyphens.
- **Prefer your name over your practice name** if you work alone. Clients
  refer to you as a person; the URL should match.
- **If your name is common**, add a city or a modality.
  `getrivet.ca/sarah-chen-emdr` is unambiguous; `getrivet.ca/john-smith`
  is probably taken.

<Tip>
  Printed materials go stale fast. Pick a name you can live with for years —
  business cards, intake emails and your website's "Book a session" link
  will all point at it.
</Tip>

## Claiming it

Tap **Save** once Rivet has marked your choice available. Your Waiting Room
then shows the full link with a **Copy** button beside it, and the "Pick
your Rivet URL" row in the setup guide ticks itself.

If you'd rather look around first, **Cancel** closes the picker; the
**Choose your URL** link on the same page brings it back.

## Changing it later

Tap **Edit URL** under your link. The first pick is free; after that you
have three changes, and the picker shows how many remain. Once they're
used up the page says so, and you can email **hello@getrivet.ca** if you
genuinely need another.

The limit is there for your clients' sake. Every change breaks the printed
card, the intake email, the calendar invite and the "Sarah's video link"
note they keep. Treat a change as a do-over you use once, early, and then
leave the address alone.

## How clients usually save it

The fastest pattern: text the URL to each client with their first
appointment confirmation. On iPhone and Android they can press and hold
the link to add it to their home screen as a one-tap icon. From then on,
"opening Sarah" is a single tap — no app, no login, no lost link.

A close second: put the URL as the "Meeting location" in the calendar
invite for each session. Most clients tap it straight from their calendar
on the day.
