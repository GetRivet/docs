description: "EMDR 2.0 inside your video session — bilateral stimulation that holds together, dual-task prompts, SUDS capture, and the 2.0 Hz safety cap."
---

EMDR lives inside the same video session as everything else. You don't pivot
to a second tool, you don't ask your client to download anything, and you
don't lose your face on screen when the stimulus is running. Tap **EMDR** in
the call toolbar, configure the set, and start.

What your client sees, what they hear, and what they feel come from one
movement rather than three things kept in step — see
[One movement, not three](/emdr/staying-in-sync).

<Warning>
  Bilateral visual stimulation is hard-capped at **2.0 Hz**. The cap holds
  wherever the speed can be set, and it can't be raised.
  See
  [Photosensitive epilepsy safety](/emdr/photosensitive-epilepsy-safety) for
  the rationale and what to screen for before your first BLS set.
</Warning>
