Rivet ships meaningful changes every 1–2 weeks. The newest items are
at the top. Tap a heading to read the article that covers that
feature in depth.

If you're new to Rivet, the [Getting started](/getting-started/index)
guide is a better starting point than this page — that's *the* tour;
this page is the running log of every change since launch.

## August 2026

### Knowing whether your text arrived {#august-2026-delivery-status}

Send a text and **Delivered** now appears beside the timestamp once the
phone network confirms it reached your client's phone. If it didn't arrive,
the message says **Not delivered** and offers to send it once more.

Before this, every text looked the same whether it landed or not —
so the only way to know was to ask the client. Now the thread tells
you.

[Knowing whether your text arrived →](/practice-phone/delivery-status)

### Client phone haptics {#august-2026-client-haptics}

Bilateral stimulation your client can *feel*. Their own phone becomes
the tactile buzzer — alternating left and right, in sync with the set
you're running — with no hardware to buy, ship, or teach anyone to
use. iPhone clients tap a link and it opens straight away; Android
clients open it in their browser. Neither installs anything.

Tactile BLS is what many therapists reach for when a client finds the
visual too activating, or when eye tracking is tiring. Until now it
meant buying tappers and getting them into the client's hands.

[Setting up an EMDR session →](/emdr/setting-up-an-emdr-session#client-phone-haptics)

### Smaller changes in August {#august-2026-smaller}

- **"Test your line" now ticks when you test by texting.** It used to
  need an inbound *call*, so a practitioner who tested by text could
  never complete that step no matter how many times she did it.
- **Starting a video session no longer loses your inbox.** The call
  opens in its own tab instead of navigating away from the
  conversation you were in.
- **Sign-in emails explain themselves.** If a link doesn't work, the
  screen now says why — open the newest email rather than asking for
  another, because each new request stops the earlier link working.

## July 2026

### EMDR session refinements {#july-2026-emdr-refinements}

SUD check-ins that fire on the protocol's own schedule rather than
your memory of it. Controls that work *during* a set — speed,
stimulus, pause — without stopping and restarting. Settings that
save per client, so someone who needs slow audio-only gets it
automatically next time. Plus VOC capture and the Flash technique
in beta.

[Setting up an EMDR session →](/emdr/setting-up-an-emdr-session)

### Sessions write to the chart on their own {#july-2026-session-chart}

What a session produces now lands on the client's record without you
copying it across. EMDR outcomes — starting and ending SUD, VOC, sets
run, modalities used — append when the session ends. Whiteboard work
saves to the chart. And a session can start a progress note that
arrives already carrying what happened, unsigned, for you to edit.

[Progress notes →](/client-record/progress-notes)

### The client record {#july-2026-client-record}

Every client now has one page: their contact details, the calls and
texts, the video sessions, the notes, the assessments they completed.
One timeline instead of four places to look.

Everything on it exports, in full, whenever you want it.

[The client record →](/client-record/overview)

### Smaller changes in July {#july-2026-smaller}

- **The waiting room knows who is actually there.** It now reflects
  whether your client is still on the page, rather than assuming they
  are for a few minutes after they check in.
- **Send from the thread.** Clinical templates and assessments go out
  from the conversation you already have open, instead of a separate
  send screen.
- **The welcome screen can be dismissed.** On phones its Continue
  button could sit off-screen, which left some practitioners stuck on
  it. It is now a sheet you can close from anywhere.

## June 2026

### EMDR 2.0 {#june-2026-emdr-2-0}

Bilateral stimulation with SUDS capture, the eight-phase session
arc, dual-task prompts that fire mid-set, and a between-sets pause
card. Built specifically for how trauma therapists actually run the
protocol — not retrofitted from a generic video tool.

[How EMDR sessions work in Rivet →](/emdr/overview)

### iOS softphone {#june-2026-ios-softphone}

The iPhone app now rings on inbound calls the same way a regular
phone call does — on the lock screen, with accept and decline.
Outbound calls dial directly from your contacts. It sounds like a
phone call, because it is one.

Pairs with the Android app, shipped earlier in the year — Rivet is
now a real phone on both platforms.

[How calling works →](/practice-phone/receiving-calls)

### In-app docs integration {#june-2026-in-app-docs}

Every settings screen in the unified app now has a small "?" icon
that opens the relevant article on this site in a new tab. Cmd+K
search inside the app surfaces docs results alongside your
conversations + contacts. Empty states have *"How [thing] works"*
links so the first time you see a quiet inbox, you know what the
surface is for.

[How help works inside Rivet →](/getting-started/index)

### Self-serve onboarding {#june-2026-self-serve-onboarding}

Sign up at [getrivet.ca/start](https://getrivet.ca/start) and have
a live practice number in 60–120 seconds. Six fields, a number
picker, and a magic-link email. No call required, no card required
— the 14-day trial starts the moment your number is live.

The previous flow was a Cal.com booking → 1:1 onboarding call →
manual provisioning. This is the same end state, but the
practitioner does it themselves in the time it takes to make coffee.

[How signup works →](/getting-started/signup)

### Call forwarding guide {#june-2026-call-forwarding}

Two patterns for routing calls from your existing line to Rivet —
manual `*72`/`*73` when you step away, or replace your carrier's
voicemail entirely with provider-specific instructions for Bell,
Rogers, TELUS, Cogeco, Shaw, Nortel, Mitel, and generic VoIP.

[How call forwarding works →](/practice-phone/call-forwarding)

## May 2026

### Pre-join experience {#may-2026-pre-join}

A camera + mic check screen before joining the call. Test your
microphone level, flip the camera off if you want, see how you'll
appear to the client. Same pattern Meet and Zoom use — Rivet now
has it too.

[The pre-join experience →](/video-sessions/the-pre-join-experience)

### Biometric unlock {#may-2026-biometric-unlock}

Face ID / Touch ID gates the app on mobile. The session is still
held inside Rivet — biometric is the daily-use convenience layer.
Toggle it on or off in Account → Security.

[Account security →](/account/managing-your-account)

### Audio-first degradation {#may-2026-audio-first-degradation}

When a video call's network gets shaky, Rivet drops video and
keeps audio rolling rather than freezing both. A small banner tells
both sides what happened, and video resumes automatically when
the connection recovers.

[Audio + video quality →](/video-sessions/audio-and-video-quality)

### Graceful rejoin {#may-2026-graceful-rejoin}

If you accidentally close the session tab — or your laptop sleeps
mid-call — re-opening it lands you back in the same call with your
camera + mic state preserved. No re-knock, no re-setup.

[Video troubleshooting →](/troubleshooting/video-issues)

## April 2026

### Per-practice waiting rooms {#april-2026-waiting-rooms}

`getrivet.ca/your-name` — one URL per practitioner. Share it once,
use it forever. Clients join from any browser, no app, no account.
You see them appear in the waiting room queue and admit them
when you're ready.

[Sharing your waiting room →](/video-sessions/sharing-with-clients)

### Clinical templates V1 {#april-2026-clinical-templates}

PHQ-9, GAD-7, and the screening-instrument library. Send a template
to a client mid-session; they fill it out on their device; the
score and responses save to the client's record. Solo-fill for
when you're administering live.

[How clinical templates work →](/clinical-templates/index)

### Whiteboard {#april-2026-whiteboard}

A shared whiteboard that lives inside the session window. Draw,
type, share preset frameworks (CBT thought records, trauma
formulation, etc.), export the whole canvas to PDF when you're
done.

[How the whiteboard works →](/whiteboard/index)

## March 2026

### Auto-reply {#march-2026-auto-reply}

Every missed call gets a text back within seconds — categorized
into one of eleven types (new client, booking, reschedule,
emergency, …) by a small local language model. The reply text is
yours; the model only picks which template fires.

[How auto-reply works →](/practice-phone/auto-response)

### Voicemail transcription {#march-2026-voicemail-transcription}

Every voicemail is transcribed automatically. The transcript shows
up under the row in the inbox; the audio recording stays available
for 30 days then is deleted. Transcripts persist beyond the audio
retention.

[How voicemail works →](/practice-phone/voicemail)
