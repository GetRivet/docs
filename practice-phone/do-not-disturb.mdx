---
title: "Do Not Disturb"
description: "One switch for time off: calls go straight to voicemail, nothing rings, and callers still hear back."
---

Turn on Do Not Disturb and your practice line stops ringing. Callers hear your greeting and can leave a message. They still get your auto-reply by text, or an away message you write. You get no calls and no notifications for calls or texts until you turn it off.

It is the switch for a vacation, a conference, a sick day, or a Sunday.

## Turning it on

Tap your avatar in the top-right corner of any screen. In the account panel, the **Do Not Disturb** card has the switch. Flip it on.

The green dot on your avatar becomes a crescent moon. That moon is how you know it's on, on every screen, the same way your phone shows a moon when its own Do Not Disturb is on.

Flip the switch off and the dot comes back. Calls ring again on the next call.

## What happens while it's on

**For a caller**

- The call goes straight to your greeting. Nothing rings.
- They can leave a voicemail. It is transcribed and lands in your **Voicemail** tab as usual.
- They get your auto-reply by text, the same one they'd get for a missed call today. If you've written an away message, they get that instead.

**For you**

- No push or text notifications for calls, voicemails or client texts.
- Waiting Room arrivals still notify you. Do Not Disturb is for the phone, and a client arriving for a session is not a phone call.
- Everything still lands in the app. Voicemails are in **Voicemail**, missed calls in **Recents**, texts in **Messages**, ready for when you're back.

## The away message

Under the switch, once it's on, there's a box for an **away message**. It replaces the text your auto-reply would normally send, for as long as Do Not Disturb is on. Each person gets it once, however many times they call or text.

Leave it empty and callers get your regular auto-reply.

Something like:

> I'm away until Oct 14. If this is an emergency, call 911, or 988 for a mental health crisis.

Up to 320 characters. Tap **Save** after you type it. When Do Not Disturb goes off, your normal auto-replies take over again, and the away message is kept for next time.

## What it doesn't change

- **Your greeting.** Callers hear the same greeting as always. If you want a different one for time off, record it in **Greeting** before you leave.
- **The Waiting Room.** Clients can still join, and you're still told when they do.
- **Anything already in progress.** A call you're on stays connected.
